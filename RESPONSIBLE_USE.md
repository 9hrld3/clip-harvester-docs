# Responsible use notice

[Español](USO_RESPONSABLE.md) · **English**

Clip Harvester is a tool for organising video material together with its credits. Using it well is up to you. Read this notice before downloading anything.

## 1. Giving credit is not the same as having permission

Naming the author and linking to the original video is good practice, but it **does not give you the right to use or publish their content**. To publish someone else's material you need one of these three things:

- permission from the rights holder,
- a license that allows it (for example, Creative Commons), following its conditions, or
- a legal exception that applies in your country.

The `CREDITOS.txt` file documents where the clip comes from. It is not an authorisation.

## 2. Downloading may breach the platforms' terms of use

YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and other platforms usually prohibit or restrict, in their terms of use, downloading content by means other than the ones they provide. Check each platform's terms before downloading. The consequences of breaching them, such as the suspension of your account, fall on you.

## 3. You declare the type of use yourself, in good faith

When downloading, you choose the type of use (`Propio`, `Edu` or `Referencia`). The tool **does not verify** that declaration: it only records it. Declare honestly:

- use `Propio` (own) only for your own material or material you hold the rights to;
- if you are not sure you can publish something, save it as `Referencia` (reference).

## 4. "Referencia" means do not publish

`Referencia` clips are marked **NO-PUBLICAR** (do not publish) in the folder name and in the credits. They are for personal reference, study or inspiration. Do not upload them, include them in your videos or share them.

## 5. "Licencia indicada" is for information only

The credits record the license the platform states for the video, when it reports one. That detail may be missing, incomplete or wrong. If you are going to publish, check the license at the original source. "No indicada" (not stated) does not mean "free to use".

## 6. Transcriptions and translations follow the same rule

The transcribed text and its translations derive from the original content. If you cannot publish the clip, you cannot publish its transcription or translation either. They are also automatic and may contain errors: review them before using them and do not attribute to anyone words they did not say.

## 7. Respect people

Do not use the tool to download private content, to harass anyone, or to reuse other people's image or voice without their consent.
